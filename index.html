Lunarcy Privacy Policy

Monkey Business Inc. · Effective November 10, 2026

Lunarcy is a game for Meta Quest headsets published by Monkey Business Inc. ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. In short: the game uses your Meta user ID and display name for leaderboards and achievements, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information on any system of our own.

Information the game uses

  • Meta user ID and display name. The game receives these from the Meta Horizon platform to confirm that you own the game, to post your scores to the leaderboard, to show the names of the players on it, and to record your achievements.
  • Gameplay results. Your score, related game statistics and achievement progress are sent to Meta's leaderboard and achievement services, where they are stored against your Meta account. Leaderboard entries, including your display name and score, are visible to other players of the game.
  • Hand and controller tracking. The game uses the position of your hands and controllers to aim and to interact with the game. This is processed on your headset while you play. It is not recorded and does not leave the headset.
  • Your surroundings in mixed reality. In mixed-reality mode the headset shows the game in your room. The game does not receive, record or transmit camera images of your surroundings.
  • Local high score. Your best score is saved on your headset so the game can show it to you.

Playing with another person

In two-player mode, two headsets on the same local network find each other and exchange game events directly, such as a match starting, a rock being pushed at the other player's base, and the condition of each base. To do this each headset announces itself on the local network, and the headsets can see each other's local network address. None of this passes through or is stored on any server of ours.

Invasions

An invasion is an attack that one player sends to another player's base, to be defended later. The game delivers invasions through Meta's leaderboard service, which it uses as a mailbox:

  • When you send an invasion, the game writes a leaderboard entry under your Meta account. The entry holds the attack plan (the rocks and rovers you chose), the deadline for defending it, and the Meta user ID of the player it is aimed at, or a mark that it is open to anyone.
  • When the invasion has been defended or has run out, the game writes a second entry under the defender's Meta account with the result: whether the base held and how many buildings were lost.
  • These entries are stored by Meta, not by us. Like other leaderboard entries, they can be read by the game on other players' headsets, and the game shows the sender's display name to the defender and the result to the sender.

An invasion contains nothing beyond the game details listed here. You can ask us to delete your invasion entries in the same way as your other leaderboard entries.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate servers that collect or store your information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking software in the game.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with third parties.
  • We do not ask for your real name, email address, contacts, location, voice or payment details.

Meta's role

Leaderboards, achievements and your Meta account are provided by Meta Platforms Technologies, and the information held in those services is handled under Meta's own privacy policies. We use that information only to run the game features described above.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep no copy of your information on our own systems. Your leaderboard entries and achievements remain in Meta's services for as long as the game offers those features or until they are deleted.

You can ask us to delete your leaderboard entries and achievement data by emailing burtsloane@gmail.com with your Meta display name. We will act on the request within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes the high score saved on your headset. You can also manage or delete your Meta account data through your Meta account settings.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information from anyone, including children. A child can play only through a Meta account set up and managed by a parent or guardian under Meta's rules. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's leaderboard and achievement data using the contact below.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what information it uses, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches players.
